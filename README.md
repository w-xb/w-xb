## 👋 About Me

I am an undergraduate student in the **2024 AI Advanced Technology Leader Class** at the **Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT)**.

I am currently a research intern at **[OrionLab](https://rshaojimmy.github.io/OrionLab/)**, advised by **Prof. Rui Shao**. My research interests center on **Vision-Language-Action models**, **world models**, and **multimodal learning for embodied intelligence**.

I am particularly interested in building intelligent agents that can:

- perceive and understand multimodal observations;
- model environment dynamics and predict future states;
- reason over instructions, goals, and interaction histories;
- translate perception and reasoning into effective actions.

---

## 🔭 Research Interests

### 🤖 Vision-Language-Action Models

Developing generalizable VLA models that connect visual-language understanding with robotic action generation, with particular interest in representation learning, action conditioning, and cross-task generalization.

### 🌍 World Models for Embodied Intelligence

Exploring predictive representations of physical environments, including future dynamics modeling, temporal reasoning, latent-state prediction, and model-based decision-making.

### 🧠 Multimodal Large Language Models

Studying multimodal perception, representation alignment, visual reasoning, and the adaptation of MLLMs to embodied agents and interactive environments.

### ⚡ Efficient Vision Systems

My previous research focused on lightweight neural networks, model compression, and efficient low-level vision, providing experience in designing resource-efficient visual models.
