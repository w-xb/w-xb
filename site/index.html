01 / ABOUT

A little about me

I study in the AI Advanced Technology Leader Class (2024) at Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT). My long-term goal is to help build intelligent systems that move beyond passive perception and can make decisions, use tools, and interact robustly with changing environments.

My current interests sit at the intersection of embodied AI and multimodal learning: how high-level reasoning can coordinate with fast action policies, how agents can recover from execution errors, and how perception can support reliable long-horizon behavior.

NTIRE 2026 · LOW-LIGHT IMAGE ENHANCEMENT

MobileIE-6Ch

An efficient, lightweight model for low-light image enhancement

A Retinex-style model developed for the NTIRE 2026 Efficient Low-Light Image Enhancement challenge. Its six-channel prediction head estimates RGB illumination and RGB residual corrections, targeting strong visual quality under tight efficiency constraints.
